### Label modifiers

A Form group with a medium-sized label. `labelClassModifiers` applies BEM
modifiers to the label, or to the legend where there is a fieldset.

<Canvas of={Stories.LabelModifiers} />


### Heading

A Form group whose label is a heading, set with `labelHeading`. With a fieldset
the heading goes inside the `<legend>`; with a label it goes around the
`<label>`, which is the only place it can validly go.

It takes any component, so a question that is the page heading asks for `"h1"`
and one that is a sub-heading on a page of several asks for `"h2"`. The label
or legend is sized to match the level, following the hierarchy GOV.UK uses on
a page that is not long form content, unless `labelClassModifiers` says
otherwise.
